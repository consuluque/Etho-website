Frequently Asked Questions

How much does it cost to join Etho?

There are no upfront costs to become a founding partner. Our planned model is commission-based, so you only pay when Etho generates a completed booking. Introductory partner terms will be confirmed during onboarding.

Can I control my pricing, availability and offers?

Yes. You decide which services to promote, when offers are available and what benefits to provide. You remain in control of your business.

Do I need to change my booking system?

No replacement is required to get started. Etho can initially showcase your services and direct enquiries to your business. We’re developing booking and payment integrations to make transactions more seamless.

How will Etho bring me new customers?

Etho connects pet parents with suitable services through personalised recommendations, exclusive benefits and targeted offers. We aim to generate bookings during quieter periods and increase the value of existing bookings.

What is agentic commerce, and why does it matter for my business?

Agentic commerce means AI can go beyond recommending services to helping customers find, book and pay for them. Etho is building the infrastructure to make pet businesses accessible to AI agents, creating a new way for customers to discover and transact with your services.
