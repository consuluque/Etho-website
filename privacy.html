Privacy Policy

Last updated 26 October 2026

Etho asks you for real details about your dog, including things you would normally only tell a vet. This policy explains what personal information we collect, how we collect it, why we use it, who we share it with, and how you can access, correct or delete it.

1. About this policy

Etho is operated by Etho Pet Health, based in New South Wales, Australia. In this policy "Etho", "we", "us" and "our" mean that operator. "You" means the person using Etho, and "your dog" means any animal you add to your account.

This policy covers our website at etho.pet, our app at app.etho.pet, our waitlist and the emails we send.

Businesses with an annual turnover of $3 million or less are generally exempt from the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth). We do not rely on that exemption. We handle your personal information in accordance with the Australian Privacy Principles (APPs). Etho is currently offered in Australia.

If you have a question about this policy, email hello@ethopethealth.com.

2. The information we collect

2.1 About you

  • Account details: your email address, and a password stored only as a cryptographic hash. We never see your password.
  • Google sign-in: if you choose it, your email address, name and profile picture from Google. We get no access to anything else in your Google account.
  • Postcode: converted to approximate coordinates so we can look up local conditions.
  • Communications: messages you send us.

We do not collect your street address, your phone number, payment details, or your device's precise location. A postcode is the most precise location information we hold.

2.2 About your dog

  • Profile: name, date of birth, breed or breeds, weight, and a photo if you add one.
  • Clinical details: known allergies and any medications.
  • Your answers: behaviour, routine, diet and preference questions you answer in the app.
  • Documents: veterinary records you choose to upload.
  • What the app produces: insights and product recommendations generated from the above.
  • Chat and notes: your messages in the Etho chat, our replies, notes you save, and notes the app writes from your chats so it can remember context between conversations.

2.3 When you join the waitlist

  • Your email address, so we can tell you when we launch and send you community invites and occasional offers.
  • How you found us: the campaign tags on the link you arrived by (utm_source, utm_medium, utm_campaign and utm_content), the site that linked to us, and the page you first landed on.
  • A starter profile, if you choose to fill it in: your name, and your dog's name, breed, age, birthday, what they love and what they struggle with.
  • Which steps of the form you complete or leave, recorded against a random number for that visit, with no cookie and nothing that links the steps to your email.

When Etho launches and you create an account with the same email, your waitlist record becomes part of that account.

2.4 Collected automatically

  • Server logs: our hosting providers keep standard logs, including IP address, browser type and the pages requested, to keep the service running and secure.
  • Cookies and browser storage: described in section 7. The Meta Pixel is the only one that needs your permission, and it stays off unless you allow it.

We run no product analytics in the app and we do not collect an advertising identifier.

3. How we collect it

  • From you, when you join the waitlist, create an account, fill in your dog's profile, upload documents, use the chat or contact us.
  • Automatically, through server logs, browser storage and, if you allow it, the Meta Pixel.
  • From Google, if you choose Sign in with Google.

You can browse our website without telling us who you are. To join the waitlist or use the app we need your email address, because we cannot provide those services anonymously.

If we receive personal information about you that we did not ask for, we will delete it unless we could lawfully have collected it ourselves.

4. How we use it

We collect and use personal information because it is reasonably necessary for the purposes below, and for purposes related to them that you would reasonably expect.

  • To create and secure your account.
  • To answer questions about your dog. Breed, age, weight, allergies, medications, behaviour answers and your saved notes give our chat assistant the context it needs to reply.
  • To recommend products. Weight, life stage, coat, energy and allergies decide which products can be shown, and allergies rule products out.
  • To show local conditions from your approximate location.
  • To generate insights from your answers over time.
  • To run the waitlist and send you updates, invites and offers.
  • To understand and improve our marketing: which links, campaigns and, if you allow the Meta Pixel, which Facebook and Instagram ads bring people to Etho.
  • To keep Etho secure, prevent misuse and respond to incidents.
  • To meet our legal obligations.

If a field is optional, it is genuinely optional; leaving it blank only means the related feature says less. We do not use your information for automated decision-making that has a legal or similarly significant effect on you.

5. Your dog's health information

The Privacy Act gives health information about people extra protection as "sensitive information". A dog is not a person under that Act, so your dog's medical history is not legally sensitive information.

We handle it as though it were. Allergies, medications and vet records sit behind the same access controls as everything else in your account, they are never sold, never shared with advertisers, and never used to build a profile of you.

Vet documents sometimes contain your own name, address or phone number. If you would rather not give us that, cover it before you upload, or skip document uploads. Nothing in the app requires them.

6. How AI is used

When you ask a question in the chat, Etho sends your message with the relevant context from your dog's record to a large language model provided by Anthropic, which processes it on our behalf and returns a reply. Uploaded vet documents are processed by the same provider to extract details for your dog's profile.

  • Your content is not used to train Anthropic's models.
  • Chat replies are AI-generated and can be inaccurate, incomplete or out of date. Etho is not a vet.

Insights and product recommendations are not generated by AI. They come from rules we wrote, checked against your dog's record, and no part of that process sends your information to an AI provider.

7. Cookies, browser storage and the Meta Pixel

Cookies and browser storage are small pieces of data kept in your browser. We use them as follows.

7.1 Essential

These are needed for the website and app to work and do not need your permission:

  • keeping you signed in to the app;
  • remembering how you first found us (the campaign tags, referring site and landing page in 2.3), stored in your browser and sent to us only if you join the waitlist;
  • remembering your cookie choice, so we do not ask again.

7.2 Advertising: the Meta Pixel

Our website can use the Meta Pixel, a tool from Meta Platforms Ireland Limited and Meta Platforms, Inc., the companies behind Facebook and Instagram. It helps us measure which of our Facebook and Instagram ads bring people to Etho, and lets Meta show our ads to people likely to be interested.

The Meta Pixel is off unless you choose Allow in the cookie banner. Nothing is loaded from or sent to Meta before then, and it is never turned on for a browser that sends a Global Privacy Control signal.

If you allow it, Meta receives:

  • the pages you visit on our website;
  • the fact that you joined the waitlist, and from which form;
  • your IP address, browser and device details, and identifiers held in Meta cookies (_fbp, and _fbc when you arrive from a Meta ad).

We never send Meta your email address, your name, or anything about your dog.

Meta may link this information to your Facebook or Instagram account if you have one, and uses it under its own terms and privacy policy, including to deliver and measure ads and to improve its products. See facebook.com/privacy/policy.

7.3 Your choices

  • Change your answer at any time with (also at the foot of our pages). Turning the Meta Pixel off stops it and deletes its cookies from our site.
  • Control how Meta uses information from other websites in your Facebook or Instagram settings, under Your activity off Meta technologies, and your ad preferences under Ad preferences.
  • Block or delete cookies in your browser settings. Blocking essential storage may stop parts of Etho working.

8. Marketing emails

If you join the waitlist, we will email you about Etho's launch, community invites and occasional offers. Every marketing email identifies us and has an unsubscribe link, and we act on it promptly, as the Spam Act 2003 (Cth) requires. You can also ask us to stop by emailing hello@ethopethealth.com.

Unsubscribing stops the emails. To remove your waitlist record entirely, ask us and we will delete it.

If you have an account we may still send operational messages about your account, security and changes to our terms or this policy.

9. Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal information.

9.1 Service providers

These providers run parts of Etho for us, under contract and on our instructions. Each sees only what it needs and may not use your information for its own purposes.

  • Supabase Database, authentication and file storage. Holds your account, your dog's details, photos, documents, chats and your waitlist record. supabase.com/privacy
  • Vercel Hosts the website and app. Sees request logs. vercel.com/legal/privacy-policy
  • Anthropic Provides the model behind the chat and document processing. anthropic.com/legal/privacy
  • Loops Our email platform. Holds your waitlist email, how you found us, and parts of your starter profile, and sends our emails. loops.so/privacy
  • Open-Meteo Weather forecasts. Receives approximate coordinates only, with nothing identifying you. open-meteo.com/en/terms

9.2 Platforms with their own policies

  • Meta, only if you allow the Meta Pixel, as described in section 7.2. Meta is an independent business that uses that information under its own privacy policy.
  • Google, if you choose Sign in with Google. Google's privacy policy governs that sign-in.
  • Retailers, when you follow a product link. Those links carry an affiliate code that identifies Etho, not you, and we may earn a commission. We do not send retailers your details or your dog's. Once you leave Etho, the retailer's own privacy policy applies, and it may set its own cookies.

9.3 Other disclosures

We may disclose information where the law requires or authorises it, to protect someone's safety, or as part of a sale, merger or restructure of our business. In that last case we will tell you, and require the successor to protect it at least as well as this policy does.

10. Overseas disclosure

Some of our providers, and Meta, store or process information outside Australia, mainly in the United States and, for Meta, Ireland. Before disclosing personal information overseas we take reasonable steps to ensure the recipient handles it consistently with the APPs. Information held overseas may be subject to that country's laws, including access by its authorities.

11. Security

  • Every database request is checked against rules that return only rows belonging to your account.
  • Photos and documents are kept in private storage, with access checked file by file.
  • Encryption in transit, and encryption at rest on our primary data store.
  • Passwords stored only as hashes.
  • Least-privilege access for anyone working on Etho, with credentials kept in secret management.

No system is perfectly secure. If a data breach is likely to cause you serious harm, we will notify you and the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC) as the Notifiable Data Breaches scheme requires.

12. How long we keep it

  • Account and dog records: while your account is open, and removed from our live systems within 30 days of deletion. We do not keep your dog's records for research after deletion.
  • Backups: purged on their own cycle, within 90 days.
  • Waitlist details: until you ask us to remove them, or, once you have an account, for as long as the account.
  • Server logs: on our hosting providers' standard schedules, typically 30 days.
  • Support correspondence: up to 24 months after the request is closed.
  • Meta Pixel data: held by Meta under its own retention policy. The _fbp cookie expires after 90 days unless renewed by a later visit, or as soon as you turn the pixel off.
  • Records we must keep by law: for the period the law requires.

When we no longer need personal information, we delete it or de-identify it.

13. Access, correction and deletion

You can ask us to:

  • access the personal information we hold about you, including what we hold about your dog under your account;
  • correct anything inaccurate, out of date or incomplete (much of it you can edit yourself in the app);
  • delete your account, your dogs and their records, or your waitlist record;
  • stop a particular use, such as marketing emails or the Meta Pixel.

Email hello@ethopethealth.com from the address we hold. We will confirm it is you, there is no charge, and we respond within 30 days. If we refuse a request, we will tell you why and how to complain.

14. Children

Etho is for adults. It is not directed at anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has given us their information, contact us and we will remove it.

15. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The current version and its date are always on this page. Where a change materially affects how we handle your information, we will tell you by email or in the app before it takes effect.

16. Complaints and contact

If you have a question, or believe we have breached the APPs, email hello@ethopethealth.com. We will acknowledge your complaint within 7 days and aim to resolve it within 30.

If you are not satisfied with our response, you can complain to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at oaic.gov.au or on 1300 363 992.
