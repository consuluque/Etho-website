2. What we collect

2.1 About you

  • Account details: your email address, and a password stored only as a cryptographic hash. We never see your password.
  • Google sign-in: if you choose it, we receive your email address, your name and your profile picture from Google. We do not receive your Google password and we get no access to anything else in your Google account.
  • Postcode: we convert it to approximate coordinates so we can look up local conditions.
  • Communications: messages you send our support address.

We do not collect your street address, your phone number, or your device's precise location. Etho has no location permission prompt. A postcode is the most precise location information we hold, and it is all the weather features need.

2.2 About your dog

  • Profile: name, date of birth, breed or breeds, weight, and a photo if you add one.
  • Clinical details: known allergies and any medications.
  • Your answers: behaviour, routine, diet and preference questions you answer in the app.
  • Documents: veterinary records you choose to upload.
  • What the app produces: the insights and product recommendations generated from the above.
  • Chat and notes: your messages in the Etho chat, our replies, notes you save, and notes the app writes from your chats so it can remember context between conversations.

2.3 Collected automatically

Our hosting providers keep standard server logs, including IP address, browser type and the pages requested. We use these to keep the service running and secure.

We run no product analytics, no advertising, no attribution tracking and no third-party cookies, on the website or in the app. We do not collect an advertising identifier. There is nothing here to opt out of, because there is nothing collecting it.

2.4 Waitlist

If you join the waitlist on our website, we collect the email address you enter so we can tell you when we launch.

3. Your dog's health information

The Privacy Act treats health information about people as sensitive information and gives it extra protection. A dog is not a person under that Act, so your dog's medical history is not legally "sensitive information".

We handle it as though it were. Allergies, medications and vet records sit behind the same access controls as everything else in your account, they are never sold, and they are never used to build a profile of you.

Because your dog's record is linked to your account, we treat the combined record as your personal information and apply this policy to all of it.

Vet documents sometimes contain your own name, address or phone number, because that is how a clinic writes them up. If you would rather not give us that, cover it before you upload, or skip document uploads entirely. Nothing in the app requires them.

4. Why we collect it

Under the Australian Privacy Principles we collect and use your personal information because it is reasonably necessary for the functions described below.

  • To create and secure your account. Email and password, or your Google sign-in.
  • To answer questions about your dog. Breed, age, weight, allergies, medications, behaviour answers and your saved notes are assembled into the context our chat assistant reads before it replies. Without them it can only speak in generalities.
  • To recommend products. Weight, life stage, coat, energy and allergies decide which products can be shown. Allergies are used to rule products out.
  • To show local conditions. Your postcode is turned into approximate coordinates and sent to a weather service.
  • To generate insights. Your answers over time feed the patterns the app surfaces.
  • To keep Etho secure and to respond to abuse or a security incident.
  • To meet legal obligations where they apply to us.

We collect what those features need and nothing further. If a field is optional in the app, it is genuinely optional, and leaving it blank only means the related feature says less.

We do not use your information for automated decision-making that has a legal or similarly significant effect on you.

5. How AI is used, and what it sees

Etho uses AI in one place only, and it is worth being precise about the boundary.

5.1 Chat

When you ask a question, Etho assembles the relevant context from your dog's profile, answers and notes, and sends it with your message to a large language model provided by Anthropic. Anthropic processes it on our behalf as our service provider and returns a reply.

  • Your content is not used to train Anthropic's models. Anthropic's commercial API terms do not permit training on the content we send.
  • Chat replies are AI-generated and can be inaccurate, incomplete or out of date.
  • The context we send is marked to show which facts you confirmed and which are assumed from your dog's breed, so the assistant does not state a guess as a fact.

Where you upload a veterinary document, its contents are processed to extract details that feed your dog's profile. That processing involves the same provider.

5.2 Insights and product recommendations

Insights and product recommendations are not generated by AI. They are produced by rules we wrote: a condition is checked against your dog's record, and if it matches, pre-written copy is shown. No part of that process sends your information to an AI provider.

This means a product cannot be recommended to your dog by a model's judgement. It is matched, or ruled out, by the details you gave us.

6. Service providers

We do not sell your information, and we do not share it for anyone else's advertising.

We use the providers below to run Etho. Each operates under contract, on our instruction, and is not permitted to use your information for its own purposes. Each sees only what it needs.

  • Supabase Database, authentication and file storage. Holds your account, your dog's details, your photos, your documents and your chats. supabase.com/privacy
  • Vercel Hosts and serves the app. Sees request logs. vercel.com/legal/privacy-policy

Google is a platform we integrate with rather than a provider acting on our instructions. If you choose Sign in with Google, Google's own privacy policy governs its handling of that sign-in.

Etho recommends products sold by other retailers. When you tap through to one, you leave Etho and that retailer's own privacy policy takes over. They may set cookies and track your visit.

These links carry an affiliate code, so we may earn a commission if you buy. The code identifies Etho, not you. We do not send retailers your details or your dog's details.

9. Security

  • Every database request is checked against row-level rules that return only rows belonging to your account. There is no query in the app that can read another owner's dog.
  • Photos and documents live in private storage, organised per account, with access checked file by file. Nothing is publicly readable.
  • Encryption in transit, and encryption at rest on our primary data store.
  • Passwords stored only as hashes.
  • Least-privilege access for anyone working on Etho, with credentials held in secret management and rotated.

No system is perfectly secure. Use a strong, unique password and keep your devices secure. If a breach occurs that is likely to cause you serious harm, we will notify you and the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner as the Notifiable Data Breaches scheme requires.

10. Deleting your account, and how long we keep things

To delete your account, email privacy@etho.pet from the address on the account. We will confirm it is you, then delete it. [Update once in-app deletion ships.]

When your account is deleted we delete your dogs, photos, documents, notes, chats, insights and recommendations. We do not retain your dog's records for research after deletion.

Retention summary

  • Active account: kept while your account is open.
  • After deletion: removed from our live systems within 30 days.
  • Backups: purged on their own cycle, within 90 days.
  • Server logs: retained by our hosting providers on their standard schedules, typically 30 days.
  • Support correspondence: up to 24 months after the request is closed.
  • Waitlist emails: until we launch, or until you ask us to remove yours.
  • Records we must keep by law: for the period the law requires.

  • Withdraw consent for optional collection, such as vet documents, by deleting them.
  • Object to a particular use, and we will explain whether we can stop it and still run the service.

Email privacy@etho.pet. There is no charge, and we respond within 30 days.

Complaints

If you believe we have breached the Australian Privacy Principles, email privacy@etho.pet. We will acknowledge within 7 days and aim to resolve it within 30. If you are not satisfied with our response, you can complain to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at oaic.gov.au.

12. Children
